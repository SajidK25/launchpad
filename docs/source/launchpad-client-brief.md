# Launchpad 

*Client requirement*

## The idea

A site where founders post their new product on a chosen day, and people vote on
it. Best products of the day sit at the top of the front page. Think of it as a
daily list of what launched today. 

We make money later from featured slots and a jobs board. Not in this version.

## Who uses it

- **Visitors.** No account. Can read everything.
- **Members.** Vote, comment, follow.
- **Founders.** Members who posted a product.
- **Reviewers.** Our staff. They approve or reject submissions.
- **Admins.** Our staff. Everything a reviewer does, plus accounts and takedowns.

## Accounts

Sign up with email and password. Google sign-in as well.

Forgot password, and change password from the settings page (if signed up with email and password).

We only want real people voting, so confirm the email address is theirs.

Each member gets a public profile page: name, photo, one line about them, their
links, and their products.

## Posting a product

The form: name, one-line pitch, description, logo, screenshots, website link, up
to three topics. They can save it and finish it later.

Our reviewers check every submission before it appears anywhere on the site.
They approve it, reject it, or send it back for changes. The founder is told the
outcome.

After approval the founder picks a launch day. It appears on the site by itself
on that day. They can change the day up until it launches. Only a set number of
products can launch on the same day for a user, two to start with.

After launch they can still edit the description and the links. Nothing else.

Nobody sees a product before its launch day.

## The front page

Today's launches, best first.

Best is not just the highest number of votes. A product that got twenty votes in
its first hour is doing better than one that got thirty across the whole day.
The order should show that, and it should keep up during the day.

Every past day keeps its own page, and the order on it never changes again.

We also want a top of the week, a top of the month, and a top of all time for
each topic.

## Voting and comments

One vote per member per product. They can take it back. No voting on your own
product.

Vote counts on screen should move on their own. No refreshing.

Comments on a product, and replies to comments. The founder is told when
somebody comments. Members can report a comment or a product, and a reviewer
handles it.

Members can follow other members and follow topics. They get a feed of what they
follow.

People will try to cheat the votes. Fake accounts, one person with ten logins,
scripts. We need to catch that.

## Notifications

Inside the site: a bell with a count, and it updates while they sit on the page.

By email:

- confirm your email, reset your password
- your product was approved, rejected, or needs changes
- reminder the day before your launch
- your product is live
- somebody commented on your product
- top five of the day, every morning
- best of the week, every Monday

Members can turn any of these off, and we must not send them anyway. If our
email provider goes down for an hour, nothing should go missing.

## Search

Search by name and by what a product does. Filter by topic. Sort by newest or
most voted. Should feel instant.

## Founder dashboard

For their own product only: views, votes over the day, where visitors came from,
and where it ranked.

They can also download all of it as a file. It can take a minute to prepare, so
let them go do something else and tell them when it is ready.

## Staff tools

- A queue of submissions waiting for review, oldest first.
- Reported comments and reported products.
- Suspend a member, take a product down, feature a product on the front page.
- A record of every action our staff took, with who and when. We will be asked
  about this.

## Other things that matter

Must work on a phone. We want a real phone app next year, and we do not want the
site rebuilt for it.

People trust us with their password and with a product nobody has seen yet.
Leaking either one ends the company.

## Not in this version

Payments. Jobs board. Private messages. Other languages. Public numbers for
products that are not yours.
